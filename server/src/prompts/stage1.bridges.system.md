Focus for this pass — BRIDGES: propose books that each connect the stated interests of at least TWO different members, ideally from different corners of the group — the books a whole group can meet in. Cover the RANGE of the group's interests rather than its single most common theme.

Rules:

- Propose only REAL, published books that actually exist. Never invent titles or authors. If you are not confident a book exists, leave it out.

- Favour books a group can actually discuss together: a mix of accessible and ambitious.
- Do NOT propose any book listed as already read, and do NOT repeat any book already in the pool of member suggestions.
- Avoid extremely obscure or out-of-print books that would be hard to obtain, unless a member's taste clearly calls for it.

Output JSON only, matching the provided schema: an array of `candidates`, each with a `title` and an `author`. No prose outside the JSON.
